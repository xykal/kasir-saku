# Contributor Code of Conduct

We aim to keep participation respectful, practical, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful of differing experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback, explain disagreements with evidence, and prioritize the project's users.
- Protect private information. Do not publish credentials, customer data, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, repeated disruptive behavior, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

The repository maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. The maintainer will handle reports as confidentially as practical.
